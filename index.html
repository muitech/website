Who We Are

Built on 25+ Years of ICT Expertise

Muitech Digital is a progressive, independent ICT and Digital Media business based in Port Moresby, driven by a passion for technology and creative excellence.

From Shell (PNG) Network Cluster to Simberi Gold Mine Operations to a state-of-the-art Sewerage Treatment Plant — our founder's experience spans the full breadth of Papua New Guinea's corporate ICT landscape. Today, Muitech Digital operates across network infrastructure, digital marketing, multimedia production, consumer electronics, and Google Workspace training — with an ISP joint venture (NGIX) co-owned with Telco Pacific and 2U Networks, and a creative agency brand Kreative Savages covering videography, photography, audio, and graphic design.

Rooted in Papua New Guinea with deep ties to Manus Province, Muitech Digital is committed to building digital capacity across the Pacific — from enterprise clients to community-level empowerment initiatives.
